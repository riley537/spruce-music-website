👋 About Me

Welcome! I'm Janice, an internationally experienced elementary teacher, Ontario music specialist, and passionate believer in the joy of music at every age. For more than a decade, I've inspired students in Ontario, Bermuda, Kuwait, and now Sechelt, blending classical training with the creative, engaging Carl Orff approach. My lessons emphasize sequential skill-building, but are always filled with fun, movement, and music for the whole self. My philosophy? Every student—regardless of age or ability—can flourish with the right encouragement and personal support. My goal is to spark confidence and a lifelong love of music in every lesson!

🎵 Classes Offered

Piano Lessons

(All Ages, All Levels)

No experience necessary — beginners to advanced, children to adults! Personalized one-on-one lessons tailored to your learning style and favourite music. Classical foundations, pop, or anything in between. Strong emphasis on creativity, enjoyment, and skill development. First lesson is always FREE! 30 minutes for first term, then 40 minutes as needed. Join as a young musician, or return as an adult — everyone's welcome.

Book a Free Lesson

Play, Move and Sing Group

(Ages 6-10, More Soon!)

Welcome, young students ages 6-10! Explore music through the proven, playful Orff method: singing, speech, creative movement, dance. Learn percussion, xylophones, and rhythm in a fun group. Build strong musical concepts through games, play, and teamwork. Orff approach supports improved literacy and math in young learners. It's great for the brain — and so much fun! (More group ages coming soon — ask to join the waitlist!)

Little Mozarts

(Ages 3-5)

A joyful music program just for preschoolers (ages 3-5), attended with a parent/adult. Based on "Little Mozarts" for the youngest musicians. Meet Mozart Mouse & Beethoven Bear. Songs, stories, puppets, rhythm activities, creative movement. Explore instruments and visual music notation. Perfect for building early love for music — and leads naturally into piano!

Why Choose Spruce Music?

At Spruce Music, every lesson is tailored to nurture a lifelong love of music in a supportive, joyful environment. With over 20 years of teaching experience, Janice is dedicated to helping children, beginners, and adults grow their musical abilities with encouragement and expertise. Whether you're learning piano, singing your first notes, or exploring music for the first time, you'll benefit from individualized attention and engaging lesson plans that make learning enjoyable and rewarding.

Located in the heart of Sechelt, Spruce Music is more than just a studio, it's a welcoming space where every student is valued, progress is celebrated, and music becomes a meaningful part of everyday life. Plus, your first lesson is absolutely free, so you can experience the difference for yourself risk-free!

Qualifications

With an international career in music education, Janice brings decades of expertise to Spruce Music Studio in Sechelt. She is an Ontario-certified music specialist and accomplished elementary teacher with extensive experience teaching in Canada and abroad. Janice's unique approach blends classical training from the Royal Conservatory of Music in Toronto with the dynamic, creative Carl Orff method. Her background includes directing school bands, choirs, and musical productions, as well as years of service as a church organist and choir director in Ontario and Bermuda. Janice is passionate about structured, sequential musical growth—always delivered with joy, creativity, and encouragement for learners of all ages and abilities.
